# Changelog

All notable changes to Pixl are listed here.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-02

First release.

- Each screen turns black on its own schedule: when the PC is idle, or when
  you haven't used that screen, even while you work on another one.
- Pick the time with a slider or type anything from 10 seconds to 24 hours;
  30 minutes by default.
- Screens fade out over 5 seconds first, and anything you do cancels it. Any
  mouse movement or key brings them back, or only moving the cursor onto
  that screen.
- Black instead of powered off: on OLED, black pixels are switched off, and a
  black screen can't leave a monitor stuck dark or move your windows. The
  cursor is hidden while a screen is black.
- A playing video keeps its screen on, including OTT services, recognised by
  their sound. Music players don't count.
- Fullscreen games and presentations, and apps on a keep-on list, keep
  screens on too.
- Shortcuts to turn every screen off, wake them all, or pause Pixl; all can
  be changed.
- Pause for 30 minutes, an hour, three hours or until you resume, from the
  window or the tray.
- Light and dark themes, accent colours, and a frosted glass or solid window.
- Starts with Windows, installs per user without admin rights, and uses no
  measurable CPU while it waits.
- Checks for a new version about once a day, shows what's new and asks
  before installing it. Only updates signed with Pixl's key are installed.
